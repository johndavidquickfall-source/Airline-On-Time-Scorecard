About the data

  • A flight counts as on time if it arrived less than 15 minutes after its scheduled time. This is the standard the U.S. Department of Transportation uses, often called A14 in the industry.
  • Rates are shares of all scheduled flights, so on time, late, cancelled and diverted add up to 100%.
  • Each airline’s figures cover only the flights it operates. Regional airlines such as SkyWest and Envoy fly for partner brands like Delta Connection and American Eagle and report their own numbers.
  • The all-carrier average weights every reporting carrier by its number of flights. A carrier appears only in years with data in the source.
  • 2020 and 2021 rates were unusually high because far fewer flights operated during the pandemic. 2026 covers January to June, the latest month available.

Source: U.S. Department of Transportation, Bureau of Transportation Statistics airline on-time performance data (TranStats), as compiled by AviationDB. Independent analysis, not affiliated with any airline.
